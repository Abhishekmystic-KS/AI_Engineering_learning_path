QUESTION
What is an AI agent?

ANSWER
An AI agent is an intelligent software program designed to achieve goals completely on its own.

Unlike standard chatbots that only answer prompts with text, an agent acts like an autonomous digital worker. It can analyze a complex problem, break it down into smaller steps, and dynamically make decisions as it works.

Crucially, it can use external tools, such as searching the web, writing code, or calling APIs, to execute tasks.

Ultimately, it is a self-correcting system that works continuously until your requested job is completely finished.
